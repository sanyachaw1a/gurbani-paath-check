How to do this (tap to open or close)
  1. Find the line in your Sanchee. Each line shows its Ang and line number.
  2. Look at the same line in the three apps. The parts that do not match have a yellow background.
  3. Tap the app that is wrong. If two are wrong, tap both. Tap again to undo.
  4. If none is wrong, press None is wrong. If you cannot tell, press I am not sure.
  5. When the page is done, press Next page at the bottom. When you finish, download your answers and send the file back.
  6. Your answers are kept as you go. The message near the top tells you where they are saved.

The Sanchee has no vishraam marks. For pauses, go by the santhya you trust, or press I am not sure. In Rehraas Sahib, Dasam Bani is not in the Sanchee, so use your Gutka for those lines.
